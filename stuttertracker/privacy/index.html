Privacy Policy — Stutter Tracker

Last updated: November 13, 2026. This policy covers the Stutter Tracker app for Android and iOS, published by Wac Labs.

Your speech data stays on your device

Recordings, profiles, notes, counts, %SS results and self-ratings are stored only on your device. We have no server for them and cannot read them. The on-device analysis runs on your phone or tablet and does not send audio anywhere. They are also excluded from automatic Android and iCloud device backups. They leave your device only when you choose to export a report or a backup, and you decide where that file goes.

Recording other people

Only record someone with their permission. If you record a child, you must be their parent or guardian or have that person's permission. Stutter Tracker is operated by adults and is not directed to children.

What is collected, and by whom

  • App usage (Google Firebase Analytics): anonymous events such as a session being saved or a report being exported, device and app version. These events never contain names, notes, recordings, transcripts, %SS or other results.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): technical details about crashes.
  • App settings (Firebase Remote Config): the app downloads settings such as update notices from Google, using an anonymous app instance ID.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): free users see ads. Google may use your device's advertising identifier and approximate location from your IP address, according to the choices you make in the consent prompt. Nothing about your speech or profiles is passed to ad networks. You can change your choice in Settings.
  • Purchases (RevenueCat, Apple, Google): an anonymous app user ID and your purchase status, used to unlock Premium.

Google's handling is described at policies.google.com/privacy, RevenueCat's at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Feedback by email

When you choose "Send feedback" in the app, or answer "Not really" to the rating prompt, the app opens your email app with a prepared message to wacteam.lab@gmail.com. The message can include an app log (technical events only: no recordings, profile names or notes) and up to 3 screenshots that you pick yourself. The app shows exactly what will be attached, and you can switch the log off or remove a screenshot before opening the email. Nothing is sent until you press send in your email app. When you send it, we receive your email address and the message, and we use them only to understand and fix the problem you report and to reply to you. We do not share them with third parties. Screenshots you pick are kept only temporarily in the app's private storage and are deleted when you leave the form or the next time the app starts.

Deleting your data

You can delete a recording, a session, a profile, or everything from within the app (Settings). Uninstalling the app removes all data stored by it, except files you exported yourself. To ask about analytics data linked to your device, or to have a feedback email deleted, email us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to withdraw consent. Contact us and we will respond.

Not a medical device

Stutter Tracker is a measuring and record-keeping tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
